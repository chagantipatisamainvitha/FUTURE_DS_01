📊 Business Sales Performance Analysis 📈

🔍 Project Overview- This project analyzes business sales performance using Excel pivot tables and charts. The objective is to understand revenue drivers, category performance, regional trends, time-based sales behavior, and the impact of higher discount levels on profitability.

📁 Dataset Description- The dataset includes transactional sales data with information on:
Products and categories
Sales and profit values
Discount levels
Regions and time periods

🛠 Tools & Techniques-
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables
Combo and column charts
Filtered analysis for focused insights

📊 Business Analysis & Insights:

1️⃣ Top Selling Products- A small set of products contributes a major share of total sales, indicating high reliance on key revenue-generating items.

2️⃣ Revenue Trends Over Time- Sales fluctuate across time periods, with noticeable peaks during certain intervals, suggesting seasonal demand or promotional influence.

3️⃣ High Value Categories- Certain categories consistently outperform others and play a critical role in total revenue contribution.

4️⃣ Region-wise Sales Performance- Sales distribution varies significantly by region, highlighting strong markets as well as regions with growth potential.

5️⃣ Impact of Higher Discounts (11%–19%)- Higher discounts drive strong sales volume but negatively affect profit margins. Several discount points show high sales with low or negative profit, indicating over-discounting risks.

🧠 Conclusion: The analysis highlights a clear trade-off between sales growth and profitability. Optimizing discount strategies and focusing on core products, categories, and regions is essential for sustainable business growth.
